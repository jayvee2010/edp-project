Key Findings

What the data tells us

Six major insights extracted from 4,600 songs across Spotify, YouTube, TikTok, Apple Music, Pandora and more.

01
🎵
English dominates
1,859 of 4,600 songs are in English — over 40% of the dataset. Spanish is the second most common language with 198 tracks.
02
📅
Friday is the best drop day
Songs released on Fridays average significantly more Spotify streams — aligning with the industry's New Music Friday standard.
03
🤝
Collabs outperform solos
671 of 4,600 songs are collaborations. The top collabs — like Post Malone ft. Morgan Wallen — consistently rank among the most streamed.
04
📱
TikTok drives discovery
Songs with high Shazam counts relative to streams show organic discovery — TikTok viral songs show this pattern most strongly.
05
🏆
Loyal fanbases win long-term
Artists with high playlist-add ratios maintain consistent streaming — playlist saves are the strongest signal of a loyal fanbase.
06
🌍
Multi-platform era arrived
Post-2021 songs appear across Spotify, YouTube AND TikTok simultaneously — the single-platform hit is increasingly rare.
